Coins that pay the people holding them.

H2E is a Solana launchpad where a coin's trading fees go to its holders — every 24 hours, in an asset each holder picks.

What goes live

  • Launch a coin and pick what its holders get paid in — a stablecoin, SOL, or a tokenized stock.
  • Payouts every 24 hours, sent to wallets. Nothing to claim.
  • Holders who want a different asset override the default for themselves.
  • Creators take no share of fees. 90% of every fee reaches a holder.
  • Every round publishes its inputs and anchors an on-chain Merkle root, so any holder can verify their own payout.
H2E is in development.
